# EasySlice.AI – FAQ & Troubleshooting

## Official FAQ Topics

### Why didn't I get clips after I connected?

**Common causes:**

1. **Brand new accounts** – If you connect brand new accounts, there is a **higher likelihood of failed runs**.  
   **Recommendation:** Warm up your account by posting videos over a few weeks, or use already active social media accounts.

2. **No new uploads** – Clips are generated when **new** videos are detected. If you connected but haven’t uploaded a new video since connecting, no clips will be created yet.

3. **Processing delay** – Detection and clipping can take a short time. If you just uploaded, wait a bit and check again.

**Official disclaimer (from EasySlice):**  
*"If you connect brand new accounts, there is a higher likelihood of failed runs. Warm up your account with videos that you post over a few weeks or use already active social media accounts to prevent this. HAPPY CLIPPING"*

### What do I do if I forget my password?

Use the **Sign in** page and look for a "Forgot password?" or similar link to reset your password via email. If you don’t see it, contact support (see Contact section below) and we can help you regain access.

### How much can I make from EasySlice?

Earnings come through **Whop’s content reward campaigns**. Typical range: **$0.50–$20 per 1K views**, with potential up to around **$20K for 1M views**. Actual amounts depend on Whop campaign terms and your content performance.

### How many clips do I get?

EasySlice creates clips **per new video** you upload. The number of clips per video is determined by the AI based on viral moments in that video. Every new video you upload is automatically processed and clipped.
