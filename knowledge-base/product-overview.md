# EasySlice.AI – Product Overview

## What is EasySlice.AI?

EasySlice.AI is an **AI-powered video clipping** application that turns new YouTube uploads into social media–ready short clips. It is fully automated: no downloading, no manual editing, no manual uploading.

**Tagline:** Automated clips. Zero effort.

## Core Value Proposition

- **Set once and forget** – Connect your channel once; EasySlice handles everything after you upload.
- **Passive income** – Clips can earn $0.50–$20 per 1K views through Whop campaigns (up to ~$20K for 1M views).
- **Time savings** – About **2 minutes** of your time per video vs **2+ hours** with manual editing.
- **Consistency** – Every new video gets clipped automatically; no dependency on your schedule.

## How It Works (4 Steps)

1. **Connect your channel** – Paste your YouTube channel URL or authenticate with Google.
2. **Detect uploads** – EasySlice detects new video uploads instantly (no refresh needed).
3. **Auto-clip with AI** – AI scans for viral moments and creates TikTok/Shorts-ready clips.
4. **Auto-post** – Clips are published to TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts.

## Supported Platforms

- **Input:** YouTube (channel connection via URL or Google auth).
- **Output:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.

## Key Differentiators vs Manual Editing

| Aspect        | EasySlice.AI     | Manual editing   |
|--------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Time per video | ~2 minutes       | 2+ hours          |
| Consistency    | 100% – every video | Variable        |
| AI analysis    | Included – finds viral moments | Manual judgment |
| Multi-platform | Automatic to all | Manual per platform |

## Technical Notes (for support)

- Uses **YouTube API** for channel and video access.
- Processing happens in EasySlice’s systems; YouTube API credentials are not shared with third parties.
- Service is designed to run passively after initial setup.
