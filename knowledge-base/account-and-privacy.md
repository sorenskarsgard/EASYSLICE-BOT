# EasySlice.AI – Account, Sign-Up & Privacy

## Sign Up & Sign In

- **Sign up:** [easyslice.ai/signup](https://www.easyslice.ai/signup) – Email and password.
- **Sign in:** [easyslice.ai/signin](https://www.easyslice.ai/signin).
- **Password reset:** Use the option on the sign-in page when available; otherwise contact support.

## Connecting Your YouTube Channel

- Users can **paste their YouTube channel URL** or **authenticate with Google**.
- Only one connection flow is needed; after that, new uploads are detected automatically.
- YouTube API is used; we do not share API credentials with third parties. Processing is done in our systems.

## Privacy (Summary for Support)

- **Data we collect:** Personal data (e.g. name, email), YouTube channel/API-related info, cookies and usage data.
- **How we use it:** To run the service (process videos, create clips), improve the product, provide support, and ensure security.
- **Data location:** Data may be transferred to and processed in the United States.
- **Privacy policy:** Full details at [easyslice.ai/privacy-policy](https://www.easyslice.ai/privacy-policy).
- **Privacy contact:** privacy@easyslice.ai.
- **Age:** Service is not intended for anyone under 18.

When users ask about data, security, or privacy, point them to the privacy policy and privacy@easyslice.ai for specifics.
